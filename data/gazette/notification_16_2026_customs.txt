MINISTRY OF FINANCE
(Department of Revenue)

Notification No. 16/2026-Customs | Dated: 12th May, 2026
G.S.R. 359(E)
================================================================================

Seeks to amend notification No. 11/2018-Customs and notification
No. 11/2021-Customs so as to revise SWS and AIDC applicable on certain items.

In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 25 of the
Customs Act, 1962 (52 of 1962) read with sections 110 of the Finance Act,
2018 (13 of 2018) and 124 of the Finance Act, 2021 (13 of 2021), the Central
Government, on being satisfied that it is necessary in the public interest so
to do, hereby amends the following notifications of the Government of India
in the Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue), as specified below:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AMENDMENT 1: Notification No. 11/2018-Customs, dated 2nd February, 2018
             (Social Welfare Surcharge — SWS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

In the said notification, in the TABLE:

(i)  against Sl. No. 1, in column (2), for the figures "7108,", the figures
     "7107, 7108, 7109, 7111, 7112," shall be substituted;
     [Effect: Expands SWS exemption coverage to heading 7107, 7109, 7111, 7112
      in addition to existing heading 7108 — SWS remains ZERO on gold bullion]

(ii) Sl. No. 54A and the entries relating thereto shall be omitted;

(iii) for Sl. No. 56A and the entries relating thereto, the following Sl. No.
      and entries shall be substituted, namely:-

      Sl. No. | Column (2)
      56A     | All goods falling under heading 7118.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AMENDMENT 2: Notification No. 11/2021-Customs, dated 1st February, 2021
             (Agriculture Infrastructure and Development Cess — AIDC)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

In the said notification:

(A) In the TABLE:

(i)   against Sl. No. 15A, in column (4), the entry "5.4%" shall be substituted;

(ii)  against Sl. No. 15B, in column (4), the entry "4.35%" shall be substituted;

(iii) against Sl. No. 15C, in column (4), the entry "4.35%" shall be substituted;

(iv)  against Sl. No. 15D, in column (4), the entry "5%" shall be substituted;
      [Effect: AIDC on gold/silver bullion (Sl. 15D) raised to 5%]

(v)  for Sl. No. 15E and the entries relating thereto, the following Sl. No.
     and entries shall be substituted, namely:-

     Sl.No. | Heading | Description                              | AIDC Rate
     15E    | 7112    | Spent catalyst or ash containing         | 4.35%
                        precious metals
                        Provided that the importer follows the
                        procedure set out in the Customs (Import
                        of Goods at Concessional Rate of Duty or
                        for Specified End Use) Rules, 2022:
                        Provided further that the importer at the
                        time and place of clearance:
                        (a) gives an undertaking to the Deputy/
                        Assistant Commissioner regarding % of
                        precious metals and that goods are imported
                        for recovery of precious metals;
                        (b) produces a certificate from the Ministry
                        of Environment, Forest and Climate Change
                        permitting import for recovery/recycling.

(vi) after Sl. No. 15E, the following Sl. No. and entries shall be inserted:

     Sl.No. | Heading | Description                         | AIDC Rate
     15EA   | 7112    | All goods other than those at 15E   | 5%

(vii) for Sl. No. 15F, the following Sl. No. and entries shall be substituted:

     Sl.No. | Heading | Description                         | AIDC Rate
     15F    | 7113    | (i)  Gold findings                  | 5%
                        (ii) Silver findings                 | 5%
                        (iii) Platinum findings              | 5.4%
                        [Explanation: findings mean hook, clasp, clamp,
                         pin, catch, screw back used to hold jewelry]

(viii) for Sl. No. 15G, the following Sl. No. and entries shall be substituted:

     Sl.No. | Heading | Description  | AIDC Rate
     15G    | 7118    | All goods    | 5%

(ix)  after Sl. No. 15G, the following Sl. Nos. and entries shall be inserted:

     Sl.No. | Heading     | Description                                | AIDC
     15H    | 7107 00 00  | All goods                                  | 5%
     15I    | 7109 00 00  | All goods                                  | 5%
     15J    | 7111 00 00  | All goods                                  | 5.4%
     15.    | 7106, 7108  | Gold and Silver covered under Sl. No. 1   | 4.35%
                            of TABLE of notification No. 57/2000-
                            Customs dated 8th May, 2000
     15K    | 71          | All goods on which BCD exemption is        | Nil
                            claimed under notification No. 57/2000-
                            Customs, other than those at Sl. No. 15K

(B) In the ANNEXURE, Sl. No. 11 and the entries relating thereto shall be omitted.

2. This notification shall come into force on the 13th day of May, 2026.

RESEARCH NOTES (gold-policy-project)
================================================================================

Key effects:
1. SWS (11/2018): Expands heading 7108 exemption to 7107, 7108, 7109, 7111, 7112.
   Gold bullion (heading 7108) retains SWS = 0%. Omits S. No. 54A.
   This CONFIRMS SWS = 0% on gold bullion both before and after May 13, 2026.

2. AIDC (11/2021): Sl. No. 15D (gold/silver bullion) raised from 1% → 5%.
   Combined with BCD 10% from Notification 15/2026:
   Total duty post-May 13 = BCD 10% + AIDC 5% + SWS 0% = 15% → DUTY_MULT = 1.15

Prior regime: AIDC was 1% (via Budget 2024, Jul 23, 2024 cut)
Prior prior regime: AIDC was 5% (before Jul 2024)
