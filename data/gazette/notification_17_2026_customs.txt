MINISTRY OF FINANCE
(Department of Revenue)

Notification No. 17/2026-Customs | Dated: 12th May, 2026
G.S.R. 356(E)
================================================================================

Seeks to amend notification No. 57/2000-Customs dated 8th May 2000, which
provides concessional rate for gold, silver and platinum imported under
specified schemes.

In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 25 of the
Customs Act, 1962 (52 of 1962), the Central Government, on being satisfied
that it is necessary in the public interest so to do, hereby makes the
following further amendments in the notification of the Government of India,
Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue) No. 57/2000-Customs, dated the
8th May, 2000, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II,
Section 3, Sub-Section (i), vide number G.S.R. 413 (E), dated the 8th May,
2000, namely:-

In the said notification, in the TABLE, against Sl. No. 1, in column (4),
for the entry "4.35%" wherever it occurs, "10%" shall be substituted.

2. This notification shall come into force on the 13th day of May, 2026.

[F. No. 354/04/2026-TRU]
DHEERAJ SHARMA, Under Secy.

Note: The principal notification No. 57/2000-Customs, dated the 8th May, 2000
was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3,
Sub-Section (i), vide number G.S.R. 413 (E), dated the 8th May, 2000 and was
last amended by notification No. 33/2024-Customs, dated 23rd July, 2024,
published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3,
Sub-Section (i), vide number G.S.R. 436 (E), dated the 23rd July, 2024.

================================================================================
BACKGROUND ON NOTIFICATION 57/2000-CUSTOMS
================================================================================

Notification No. 57/2000-Customs (dated 8 May 2000) grants concessional
customs duty rates on gold, silver, and platinum imported under specified
import schemes through:
  - Nominated agencies (e.g. MMTC, SBI, designated banks)
  - Qualified importers under export-oriented or trade-linked schemes
  - Star Export Houses and others with DGFT approval

The concessional rate was:
  - Pre-Budget 2024: 4% (then revised to various rates)
  - Post-Jul 23, 2024 (Notification 33/2024): 4.35%
  - Post-May 13, 2026 (THIS NOTIFICATION): 10%

This is separate from the main BCD notification (45/2025). The concessional
import route is used by nominated banks/agencies importing for domestic supply.

================================================================================
RESEARCH NOTES (gold-policy-project)
================================================================================

Key effect: Concessional duty for banks/nominated agencies importing gold
under Notification 57/2000 raised from 4.35% → 10%.

Last amended by Notification 33/2024-Customs, dated 23 July 2024 — this was
the Budget 2024 duty cut that reduced BCD from 10% to 5% (and concessional
rate from some higher level to 4.35%). This confirms the July 2024 cut date
as 2024-07-23 (DUTY_CUT_DATE in the analysis).

For the ITS analysis: the concessional route is the primary import channel for
bullion banks (e.g. HDFC, Axis, ICICI, SBI). So the effective BCD for standard
imports was 5% (Jul 2024-May 2026) and is now 10%, matching our DUTY_MULT.
