GAZETTE NOTIFICATIONS — GOLD IMPORT DUTY HIKE (MAY 13, 2026)
data/gazette/
================================================================================

This directory contains the verbatim text of the four CBIC customs notifications
that collectively raised India's gold import duty from 6% to 15%, effective
13 May 2026. Text retrieved from TaxGuru.in (which reproduces official gazette
content), since the CBIC eGazette portal requires JavaScript to access PDFs.

FILES
-----
notification_15_2026_customs.txt    BCD raised 5% → 10% on gold/silver/precious
                                    metals (amends No. 45/2025-Customs)
                                    G.S.R. 358(E) | Key: S.Nos 192-197, 203

notification_16_2026_customs.txt    SWS and AIDC revised
                                    (amends No. 11/2018-Customs and 11/2021-Customs)
                                    G.S.R. 359(E) | Key: AIDC Sl. 15D raised 1% → 5%
                                                         SWS on 7108 confirmed = 0%

notification_17_2026_customs.txt    Concessional duty raised 4.35% → 10%
                                    (amends No. 57/2000-Customs)
                                    G.S.R. 356(E) | Key: nominated agency imports

notification_18_2026_customs.txt    India-UAE CEPA preferential rates revised
                                    (amends No. 22/2022-Customs)
                                    G.S.R. 357(E) | Key: closes UAE re-export loophole
