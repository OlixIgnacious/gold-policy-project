HIGHLIGHTS (verbatim from article):
- Gold import duty was raised sharply by 9% — from 6% to 15%, the steepest increase on record — alongside broader regulatory tightening
- Domestic gold prices have not yet fully reflected the duty hike amid weak demand and ample supply; local markets are currently in deep discount from the landed price
- Past trends indicate that higher duty increases unofficial inflows, although official imports remain relatively resilient
- Gold demand is expected to moderate in 2026, with jewellery and bar and coin demand projected to decline by 50–60t (~10% y/y) on account of the import duty hike

KEY DATA POINTS CITED IN ARTICLE:

1. PRE-HIKE DISCOUNT (week prior to May 13):
   "discount widened from an average of US$14/oz the week prior to the duty hike"
   → Our dataset validation: May 12 discount = ₹433/10g = 14.1 USD/oz ✓ EXACT MATCH

2. POST-HIKE PRICE RISE:
   "Physical market prices, proxied by the MCX spot gold price, have risen in the range of 4% to 6% since the change in duty"
   → Our dataset: May 12 IBJA 151,632 → May 13 IBJA 160,977 = +6.2% ✓

3. POST-HIKE DISCOUNT TO LANDED PRICE (as of May 18):
   "widening from an average of US$14/oz the week prior to the duty hike to nearly US$150/oz"
   → Our dataset: May 13 discount to 15% parity ≈ 164 USD/oz (vs NCDEX/LBMA used by WGC — small diff expected)

4. APRIL 2026 IMPORTS:
   "April imports rose to US$5.6bn, up more than 80% on an annual as well as a sequential basis"
   → Estimated volume: 48–55 tonnes (front-loading ahead of duty hike)

5. DEMAND FORECAST 2026:
   "combined jewellery and bar and coin demand could decline by around 50–60t, around 10% lower than the previous year"

6. GOLD ETF FLOWS (April 2026):
   Net inflows: INR 30.4bn (US$325mn) — 12th consecutive month of positive flows
   Holdings: 116.7t | AUM: INR 1,781bn (US$19bn)
   Folios: 12.5 million accounts

7. SMUGGLING CORRELATION:
   "correlation between import duty and unofficial imports is positive at 0.52"
   After 2013 hike: unofficial imports rose from ~10t (Q1 2013) to 70t (Q1 2014)
   After July 2024 duty cut to 6%: unofficial imports fell to near zero

8. HISTORICAL IMPORT RESILIENCE:
   "official imports remained relatively resilient, between 175t and 236t per quarter in most periods"
   Correlation between duty rates and official imports: −0.17 (weak, negative)

9. SHARE PRICE IMPACT:
   "Share prices of listed jewellers fell by ~2%–17% following the duty hike"

10. UAE CEPA / ADVANCE AUTHORISATION:
    "Rules were also tightened for gold imports linked to exports (under the advance authorisation scheme)"
    Cap set at 100 kg (Tribune India, 20 May 2026)

11. PM APPEAL TO CONSUMERS:
    "the Prime Minister has directly appealed to consumers, urging them to avoid buying gold for a year"
    (India Today, 11 May 2026)

12. BANKS PAUSE:
    "banks pausing bullion imports for over a month" pending IGST exemption notification
    (MoneyControl, 12 May 2026)
